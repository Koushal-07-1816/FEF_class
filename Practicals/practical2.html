Welcome to Aarogya Hospital

Aarogya Multispeciality Hospital provides medical services with the aim of improving the health and well-being of patients. Our hospital offers consultations and treatment in different medical departments.

Our Facilities

  • Patient consultation
  • Medical laboratory
  • Emergency services
  • Pharmacy

Medical Departments

  1. General Medicine

    Provides consultation for common illnesses and general health problems.

  2. Cardiology

    Focuses on heart health and cardiovascular conditions.

  3. Pediatrics

    Provides medical care and treatment for children.

  4. Orthopedics

    Helps patients with bone, joint and muscle problems.
